## 1. Analytics & Metrics

`ExportHistory` records all executed export jobs and computes statistics:

- **Total Volume**: Aggregates total exported byte volume.
- **Top Format**: Identifies most frequently selected export format.
- **Average Duration**: Calculates average export job execution latency in milliseconds.
